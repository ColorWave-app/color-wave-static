# Privacy Policy for Color Wave

**Effective date: September 3, 2026**

Color Wave is a mobile puzzle game published by Maxime Joassy, established in
France. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use
Color Wave.

## 1. Information Stored by Color Wave

Color Wave does not require a user account and does not ask you to provide your
name, postal address, phone number, contacts, or precise location.

The app stores the following information locally:

- Identifiers of completed levels, to save game progress.
- A completion counter, used to determine when an interstitial advertisement
  may be displayed.

This information is not transmitted to Maxime Joassy or stored on his servers.

It remains on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
Depending on your operating system settings, it may be included in backups
managed by your device or cloud provider. Maxime Joassy does not have access to
those backups.

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the contents of
your message and use them to respond to your request.

## 2. Google Mobile Ads

Color Wave uses Google Mobile Ads, also known as AdMob, to display banner and
interstitial advertisements.

When an advertisement is requested or displayed, the Google Mobile Ads SDK
collects and shares certain information with Google. According to Google, this
may include:

- Your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate your general
  location.
- Interactions with the app and advertisements, such as app launches, taps, and
  ad views.
- Diagnostic information about the app and advertising SDK.
- Device or account identifiers, including the advertising ID and App Set ID
  where available and permitted.

Google uses this information for advertising, performance measurement,
analytics, security, and fraud prevention. Google states that information
collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS.

Google processes this information according to its own privacy practices:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

## 3. Children and Advertising

The target audience of Color Wave includes children under the age of 13.

Advertising requests are configured for child-directed treatment, and Color
Wave requests non-personalized advertisements.

Non-personalized advertisements are not selected using a profile of the user's
previous behavior. They may still use contextual information, such as the
app's content or general location. Google may also process limited information
for advertisement delivery, frequency management, measurement, security, and
fraud prevention.

Color Wave does not provide account creation, chat, or public posting features
and does not knowingly ask children to submit personal information.

If you believe that a child has sent personal information to us by email,
please contact us so that we can review and delete it where appropriate.

## 4. Purposes and Legal Bases

Local gameplay information is used only to save game progress and manage
advertisement timing.

Advertising helps fund the availability of Color Wave. Information processed
through Google Mobile Ads is used for child-directed, non-personalized
advertising, measurement, security, and fraud prevention.

Where applicable law requires consent before advertising technologies access
or store information on a device, those technologies must not be used until the
required choice has been obtained.

Google describes the legal bases it relies upon in its own Privacy Policy.

## 5. Sharing of Information

Color Wave shares information with Google through the Google Mobile Ads SDK as
described above.

Maxime Joassy does not operate a backend that receives gameplay progress and
does not use an independent analytics or crash-reporting service in Color Wave.

## 6. Retention and Deletion

Local gameplay information remains on your device until you clear Color Wave's
app data or uninstall the app. Maxime Joassy does not retain a server-side copy.

Google determines the retention period for information processed through
Google Mobile Ads. Please consult Google's Privacy Policy for details about its
retention practices and available controls.

## 7. International Processing

Google may process information on servers located outside your country. The
safeguards used by Google for international transfers are described in Google's
Privacy Policy.

## 8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning your personal
information, including rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction,
objection, portability, and withdrawal of consent.

Because gameplay information is stored locally, you can delete it by clearing
the app's data or uninstalling Color Wave.

For information processed by Google, you can use Google's privacy controls or
contact Google as described in its Privacy Policy.

For questions or requests relating to Color Wave, contact
[pifaacedev@gmail.com](mailto:pifaacedev@gmail.com).

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you may lodge a complaint
with your local data protection authority. In France, the relevant authority is
the [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr/).

## 9. Security

Color Wave limits its own storage to the local gameplay information described
above. Google states that information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is
encrypted while being transmitted.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be
completely secure.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Color Wave's features, service providers,
or legal obligations change. When it is updated, the effective date shown at
the top will also be updated.
