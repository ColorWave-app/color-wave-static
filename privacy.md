# Privacy Policy for Color Wave

**Effective date: October 10, 2026**

Color Wave is a mobile puzzle game published by Maxime Joassy, established in
France. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use
Color Wave.

## 1. Information Stored on Your Device

Color Wave does not require a user account and does not ask you to provide your
name, postal address, phone number, contacts, or precise location.

The app stores the following information locally on your device:

- Identifiers of completed levels, to save game progress.
- A completion counter, used to determine when an interstitial advertisement
  may be displayed.
- Your Firebase Analytics choice, the version of the privacy notice associated
  with that choice, and the date of the choice.
- Advertising privacy choices managed by Google's User Messaging Platform
  (UMP), where required.

Gameplay progress and the interstitial counter are not transmitted to Maxime
Joassy or stored on his servers. Local information remains on your device until
you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Depending on your operating
system settings, it may be included in backups managed by your device or cloud
provider. Maxime Joassy does not have access to those backups.

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the contents of
your message and use them to respond to your request.

## 2. Firebase Analytics

Color Wave uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used
and to improve the game. Analytics collection is disabled by default. On first
launch, the app asks whether you agree to audience measurement. No Analytics
data is collected by Color Wave unless you accept.

When Analytics is enabled, the Firebase Analytics SDK may automatically collect
and send information to Google, including:

- App interactions and usage events, such as app launches, sessions, and time
  spent using the app.
- App and device information, such as the app version, device type, operating
  system, language, and general technical characteristics.
- An app-instance identifier and related technical identifiers used to
  distinguish app installations and measure usage.
- General location information derived from the IP address, such as country or
  region. Color Wave does not request access to your device's precise location.

Color Wave does not send your name, email address, gameplay grids, or completed
level identifiers to Firebase Analytics and does not set a user ID or custom
user properties.

You can enable or disable Analytics at any time in Color Wave's privacy
settings. If you disable it, the app stops Analytics collection and asks the SDK
to reset Analytics data and the app-instance identifier stored on the device.
This does not necessarily delete information that Google previously processed,
including aggregated information. Google handles that information according to
its terms and retention settings.

For more information, see:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Privacy and security in Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

## 3. Google Mobile Ads

Color Wave uses Google Mobile Ads, also known as AdMob, to display banner and
interstitial advertisements. The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to
request updated advertising privacy information at each launch, show a privacy
message when required, and determine whether advertisements may be requested.
Color Wave does not request an advertisement unless Google's SDK indicates that
advertising is permitted.

Depending on your location, applicable law, age-related requirements, and the
choices available in Google's privacy message, Google may serve personalized,
non-personalized, or limited advertisements. You can reopen Google's privacy
options from Color Wave's privacy settings whenever Google requires that option
to be available.

When an advertisement is requested or displayed, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may
collect and share information with Google, including:

- Your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate your general
  location.
- Interactions with the app and advertisements, such as app launches, taps, ad
  requests, ad views, and impressions.
- Diagnostic and performance information about the app, device, and
  advertising SDK.
- Device or account identifiers, including the advertising ID and App Set ID
  where available, permitted, and not restricted by the applicable advertising
  mode.

Google uses this information to provide and measure advertisements, manage ad
frequency, maintain security, and prevent fraud and abuse. Google states that
information collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS.

For more information, see:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Google advertising technologies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)

## 4. Children and Advertising

The target audience of Color Wave includes children under the age of 13. Color
Wave does not provide account creation, chat, or public posting features and
does not knowingly ask children to submit personal information.

Advertising for users treated as children or as under the applicable age of
consent is subject to Google's age-related restrictions. These restrictions may
limit ad personalization, identifiers, and third-party ad vendors. A parent or
guardian should review and make the privacy choices presented in the app for a
child who cannot legally make those choices.

If you believe that a child has sent personal information to us by email,
please contact us so that we can review and delete it where appropriate.

## 5. Purposes and Legal Bases

Local gameplay information is used only to save game progress and manage
advertisement timing.

Firebase Analytics is used for audience measurement and improvement of the app
only after you consent. You may withdraw that consent at any time in the app's
privacy settings without affecting the lawfulness of processing that occurred
before withdrawal.

Advertising helps fund the availability of Color Wave. Google Mobile Ads uses
information for ad delivery, measurement, frequency management, security, and
fraud prevention. Where applicable law requires consent before advertising
technologies access or store information on a device, Color Wave relies on
Google's UMP flow and does not request advertisements until the Google SDK
indicates that it may do so. Google describes the legal bases it relies upon in
its own Privacy Policy.

## 6. Sharing and Service Providers

Color Wave shares Analytics information with Google only when Analytics has
been enabled. It also shares information with Google through the Google Mobile
Ads SDK when advertisements are permitted and requested, as described above.

Maxime Joassy does not operate a backend that receives gameplay progress and
does not use an independent crash-reporting service in Color Wave.

## 7. Retention and Deletion

Local gameplay information and privacy choices remain on your device until you
clear Color Wave's app data or uninstall the app. Maxime Joassy does not retain
a server-side copy of that information.

Google determines the retention periods for information processed through
Firebase Analytics, Google Mobile Ads, and UMP. Google Analytics retention
settings apply to user-level and event-level data, while some aggregated reports
may be retained for longer. Please consult Google's policies for current
retention practices and controls.

Disabling Analytics in Color Wave stops future Analytics collection and resets
Analytics data stored by the SDK on the device. You may also clear all local app
data by using your operating system settings or uninstalling the app.

## 8. International Processing

Google may process information on servers located outside your country. The
safeguards used by Google for international transfers are described in Google's
Privacy Policy and applicable data-processing terms.

## 9. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning your personal
information, including rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction,
objection, portability, and withdrawal of consent.

You can manage Firebase Analytics and, when required, Google advertising privacy
choices from Color Wave's privacy settings. Because gameplay information is
stored locally, you can delete it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling
Color Wave.

For information processed by Google, you can use Google's privacy and
advertising controls or contact Google as described in its Privacy Policy.

For questions or requests relating to Color Wave, contact
[support@color-wave.fr](mailto:support@color-wave.fr).

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you may lodge a complaint
with your local data protection authority. In France, the relevant authority is
the [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr/).

## 10. Security

Color Wave limits its own storage to the local information described above.
Google states that information collected by Firebase and the Google Mobile Ads
SDK is encrypted while being transmitted.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be
completely secure.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Color Wave's features, service providers,
or legal obligations change. When it is updated, the effective date shown at
the top will also be updated. If a change requires a new consent choice, the app
may ask you to make that choice again.
